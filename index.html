About this project

This student project helps residents, students, older adults and visitors find nearby essential public services. Select a category to open a Google Maps search and compare nearby options.

Before travelling: Map results can include nearby areas and may change. Confirm the exact address, phone number, opening hours and availability from a reliable listing. This directory is a student reference tool, not an official government service or emergency dispatch system.
